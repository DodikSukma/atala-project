Slide 5: Penjumlahan & Kesimpulan
Pertanyaan: Total luas atap = 36 + 60 = ? m²
Jawaban: 96

Perhitungan: 36 + 60 = 96 m²
Ringkasan Perhitungan Lengkap:
Luas alas = a × a = 6 × 6 = 36 m²
Keliling alas = 4 × a = 4 × 6 = 24 m
Luas semua sisi miring = 1/2 × Keliling alas × s = 1/2 × 24 × 5 = 60 m²
Total luas permukaan atap = Luas alas + Luas sisi miring = 36 + 60 = 96 m²
